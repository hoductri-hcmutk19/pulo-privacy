Privacy Policy

Pulo app · Tri Studio · Effective 4 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no server. All your data stays on your phone.
  • The developer does not collect your measurements, breathing sessions or any other data in the app.
  • No ads, no analytics, no SDK that sends data out.
  • Camera images are used only to estimate your pulse and are discarded at once; they are never saved or sent anywhere.

1. Who is responsible

Pulo is developed by Tri Studio. For any privacy question, write to tristudio.contact.hdt@gmail.com.

2. Data the app keeps on your phone

To show your history, charts and a comparison with your usual resting level, the app stores:

  • Pulse checks: time, estimated pulse, confidence, the context you pick (for example "Resting") and the time of each beat during the check. These are computed numbers, not images or video.
  • Breathing sessions: time, exercise, duration, breathing pace, and the calm level you rate before and after.
  • Settings: theme, language, sound, vibration, reminder times, your breathing pace, exercises you create, progress in the 7-day program, Pro status.

This data lives in the app's private storage. Android does not let other apps read it.

3. Camera and flash

The app asks for camera access only when you start your first pulse check. During a check the flash shines through your fingertip; each frame is reduced to a few brightness numbers in memory and then discarded. The app never saves photos or video and never sends frames anywhere. If you decline camera access, every breathing exercise still works.

4. Backup

The app uses Android's automatic backup. The system backs up your history and settings to your own Google account and restores them when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. The backup is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer cannot access it. You can turn backup off in Android's backup settings.

5. Buying Pro

Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app never sees your card or payment account, only whether Pro has been bought. The app has internet access only for this. It sends none of your health or personal data over the network.

6. Other features

  • Reminders: scheduled and shown on your phone, without any server.
  • Voice cues: the app uses only offline text-to-speech voices on your phone, never voices that need the network.
  • Emergency number: when the app suggests an emergency number, it reads the country code from your SIM, mobile network or language settings to pick the right one. The code is used only on your phone. The app does not use GPS location.
  • Sharing: only when you tap share, the app makes an image of your result card and opens Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes; once sent, the receiving app's policy applies. You can also save the image to your photo album.
  • Feedback by email: the app opens your email app. If you send a message, we receive your email address and what you wrote, use it only to reply to you, and share it with no one. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

7. What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data.
  • It has no ads and no third-party analytics or crash reporting.
  • It does not read your contacts, photos, location or other apps' data.
  • It does not connect to Health Connect or any other health service.

8. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all data removes every pulse check, breathing session and setting; the app returns to a fresh install. Pro stays, because Google Play keeps it.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data on your phone.
  • The backup in your Google account: delete it from the backups section of Google Drive or Google One.

9. Age

Pulo is meant for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. The app collects no one's data. If you learn that a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete that message.

10. Health note

Pulo is a relaxation app, not a medical device. The pulse measured with the camera is an estimate for reference only; accuracy depends on the device.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date at the top. Important changes are also noted in the "What's new" section of the update on Google Play.
